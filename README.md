# palabrapal-releases

📚 PalabraPal: Spanish vocabulary, all in one place

Build a real Spanish vocabulary, entirely offline, entirely on your PC.

PalabraPal is an all-in-one Spanish learning app for English speakers who want to really know their words. Inspired by Anki and taking inspiration from the clean, reading-first design of MandaChinese, PalabraPal puts it all and more in one fast desktop app. You don’t need an account or a subscription, or even an internet connection. 🔒

✨ Why PalabraPal?

🧠 Remember more and review less. Reviews are scheduled with FSRS, the memory model Anki now uses by default. Each word learns its own strength and difficulty from your answers, so it comes back just before you’d forget it.

📖 A complete dictionary. More than 111,000 entries, with clear meanings, real example sentences, CEFR levels (A1–C2), frequency ranks and full verb conjugations. Search in Spanish or English, and accents are optional.

📴 100% offline and local. Everything runs on your computer, including the dictionary, the translator and text recognition. Your words and progress stay on your PC and are never uploaded.

🗂️ What’s inside

🃏 Flashcards and review

A smart daily review queue with new-word and review limits
Four card types: Spanish → English, English → Spanish, listening, or mixed
A memory graph for every word, showing recall now, the next review and how well the word is holding
An end-of-session summary showing what you mastered and what gave you trouble
Export your decks to Anki (.apkg) whenever you like

📕 Read real Spanish

101 graded texts, all levelled A1–C2: stories, news-style articles and full books
Click any word to see its meaning and save it in one click
Press R to translate the whole page. It’s instant for the built-in texts, and works for your own texts with the offline translator
Import your own texts, subtitles (.srt), e-books (.epub) or photos of a page 📸
Read aloud with word-by-word highlighting

🛤️ Learning Path

76 themed units across six sections, from “Hello and goodbye” 👋 to “Literary Spanish” 🪶
Short lessons: learn the words, match them, then review the ones you missed ⭐
An adaptive placement quiz tells you where to start

🎯 Practice modes

✏️ Cloze (fill in the missing word)
📄 Passage reading
♂️♀️ Gender drill (el or la?)
⚡ Speed reading

📋 Word lists and more

260+ topic word lists, from food 🍎 and travel ✈️ to law ⚖️ and astronomy 🔭
CEFR level lists, so you can see exactly where your vocabulary stands
Dichos Database: Spanish idioms, sayings and slang 💬
Your own custom lists and notes

A 7-day, 30-day and 3-month review forecast
